# About taotao

This is a personal notebook for robotics, AI, and the questions in between.

I use it to turn learning, practice, and making into work that can be revisited, tested, and revised. The notes here are written for myself first, and left open in case they become useful to someone else.

## What you will find here

Each entry starts with one coherent thing: a specific question, a complete project, a failed attempt, an observation, or an idea that needs more time. The form changes with the subject. The goal is to leave enough context for the work to make sense later.

The subjects will change too. The current articles are a snapshot, not a boundary.

## Why keep it public

Writing makes thinking more precise. Publishing leaves room for another person to follow the thread, challenge an assumption, or reuse something that proves useful.

This is not a claim to expertise. Confidence is built gradually through understanding, making, testing, and returning to what is still unclear.

## A living record

Entries may be revised when the understanding behind them materially changes. The timeline reflects substantial revisions; ordinary edits do not rewrite the history of a note.
