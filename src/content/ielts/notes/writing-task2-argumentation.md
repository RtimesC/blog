## 一、为什么论证容易被扣分？

在 TR（Task Achievement / Task Response）和 CC（Coherence & Cohesion）中，常见的失分现象是：
- **Point Listing（观点罗列）**：写了 3~4 个点，但每个点都只写了一句话，没有深入解释因果链条。
- **逻辑跳跃（Logical Leap）**：从“使用手机”直接得出“社会道德滑坡”，中间缺乏推导过程。
- **例子泛滥或虚假编造**：写不出道理，只能编造“According to a survey by Oxford University in 2020...”。

---

## 二、PEEL 论证模型规范

一个严密的主体段（Body Paragraph）通常由 **4 到 5 句话** 构成：

| 步骤 | 角色 | 作用 | 常用引导词 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P** | Point | 主题句：清晰指出本段的核心论点 | *The primary reason is that... / One major benefit is...* |
| **E** | Explanation | 因果解释：说明该论点为什么成立、机制是什么 | *This is because... / In other words, when...* |
| **E** | Example | 具象化举例/场景展开：用具体生活场景或事实支撑 | *For example, ... / A clear instance is...* |
| **L** | Link | 总结/回扣：说明带来的结果并回扣段落或题目立场 | *As a result, ... / Consequently, this leads to...* |

## 三、实战演练对比

### ❌ 不合格写法（观点罗列、无展开）
> Fast food is bad for health. People eat too much junk food and get sick. Also, fast food contains a lot of sugar and fat. So governments should tax it.

### ✅ 合格写法（PEEL 结构完整）
> **[Point]** One major reason to impose taxes on unhealthy food is to mitigate the rising burden on public healthcare systems.  
> **[Explanation]** Highly processed meals and sugary beverages typically contain excessive saturated fats, which significantly elevate the risk of chronic conditions such as obesity and cardiovascular diseases among regular consumers.  
> **[Example]** For instance, in countries where heavy taxes were introduced on sugary drinks, consumption levels dropped noticeably within two years.  
> **[Link]** Consequently, financial disincentives not only deter harmful dietary habits but also generate dedicated public funds to treat lifestyle-related illnesses.

## 四、自查清单 (Revision Checklist)

1. 段落首句是否能直接回答题目？
2. 是否包含完整的因果机制（Why / How）？
3. 最后的结论是否自然闭合，没有跑题？
